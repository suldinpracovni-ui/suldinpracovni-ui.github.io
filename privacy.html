Privacy Policy

Max Content Poster is a private tool. Only its owner, Max Suldin, uses it, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

It only accesses the accounts Max connects, and only to publish the videos he picks. It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone else.

Login tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to publish. Access can be removed at any time from the Google or Instagram account settings.
